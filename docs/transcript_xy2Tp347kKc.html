Why It’s So Important Not To Judge Others


Transcript

There is a game that people play all the time. They play God. And how do they do it? Because they're judging others. When you judge somebody else, you're playing God. That's not our job. But Satan loves it when you judge because then you're doing his work. So I don't want to do his work.

We don't know what people have been going through and I think once you get rid of that judging and trying to figure out why people do what they do, because we'll never know. But that's God's business. If today's world knew that and could live even a little bit like that, I feel like the whole world would be in such a battle.

It would be such a better place.
