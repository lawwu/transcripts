Where Hoodie Melo Came From


Transcript

The hoodie Melo thing, how that came about. I remember Melo was going through a time in New York where playing for the Knicks isn't easy. You're not winning championships, which they haven't been, and you're the franchise player. They're going to be on you, especially if you're not making big playoff runs.

When we were in the gym, he didn't want to be bothered. He didn't want anyone coming up to him. Hey, Melo, come on. So he put a hood on, and that was him being like, don't talk to me. We just started working out, and we were posting, and he had the hood on, and then that just became a thing.

I think the NBA implemented hoods that next season.
